Web app

Use it right here

The full extension, running in this browser tab: wallet scores, wallet lists, Top traders, Holders, Automatic and History. Add your free Helius key in the menu (three lines, top right) to start.

Before you start

  1. Get a free key at helius.dev (API Keys).
  2. Open the menu in the app (three lines, top right) and paste it under Helius API keys.
  3. Paste a wallet, or a list of wallets, and press Score.

Good to know

  • Your key and results are saved in this browser only, for this site. Nothing goes to a server of ours.
  • Same Helius credits as the extension: about 100+ per new wallet.
  • Keep this tab open while a scan runs.

Extension only

  • Score badge and labels on GMGN wallet pages
  • Auto-checking the wallet or token page you have open
  • Side panel next to any site

Get the extension

Data saved by the extension and by the web app is separate; each keeps its own history.
