Privacy policy

Copytrade Wallet Score analyzes public Solana blockchain data in your browser. The developer runs no server and collects nothing: no account, no analytics, no tracking, no ads. Everything you enter or the extension works out stays in your own browser, except the lookups it sends to the data services listed below to do its job.

What the extension handles

  • Your Helius API key or keys. You paste these in Settings. They are saved in your browser and sent only to Helius, as part of each data request.
  • Wallet and token addresses. The addresses you type or paste, and the ones found while scanning a token's traders or holders. These are public blockchain addresses.
  • Analysis results. Scores, filters, trade histories pulled from the blockchain, token names and prices, saved Holders checks and your History list.
  • Your settings. Scoring rules, filters and display choices.

The extension does not ask for or read your name, email, browsing history, passwords, private keys or seed phrases. It never connects to or signs with a crypto wallet and cannot move funds.

Services it contacts

GMGN pages

On gmgn.ai the extension reads the address of the page you are on and the token links on it, so it can show a score badge and labels for wallets you analyzed. It does not click, type, submit or change anything on GMGN, and nothing from the page is sent anywhere. Turn it off in Settings → Analysis → Show Wallet Score on GMGN pages.

The web app

The web app on this site runs the same analysis in your browser tab. It works the same way: your Helius key, wallets and results are saved in this site's storage in your browser (localStorage) and sent only to the data services above. This website has no server-side code, no analytics and no cookies. Clearing this site's data in your browser deletes everything the web app saved.

Storage and deletion

  • All data is kept in your browser's extension storage on your device; it is not synced to the developer or shared.
  • Trades older than 31 days are removed automatically.
  • History → Clear history removes saved wallets, scores and Holders checks. Settings → Storage clears each kind of data.
  • Removing the extension from Chrome deletes everything it saved.

Permissions

  • Side panel: shows the extension next to the page you are browsing.
  • Storage and unlimited storage: keep your settings, keys and saved trade history on your device so reopening a wallet is free.
  • Access to the sites above: the three data services, plus gmgn.ai for the page labels and to read which wallet or token page you have open.

Limited use

The use of information received by Copytrade Wallet Score adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to provide the extension's wallet analysis. It is never sold, never transferred to third parties except the data services above as needed for that purpose, never used for advertising, and never used to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

Other terms

  • The extension is an analysis tool. Its scores and filters are estimates from past trades, not financial advice.
  • It is not affiliated with GMGN, Helius, GeckoTerminal or Binance.US.
  • If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, use the Support tab on the extension's Chrome Web Store page.
